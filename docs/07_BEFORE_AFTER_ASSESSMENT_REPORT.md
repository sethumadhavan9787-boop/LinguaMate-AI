### 3. Qualitative Error Reduction Analysis

#### A. Mother-Tongue (L1) Interference Elimination
- **Day 1 Pattern:** 82% of learners opened introductions with *"Myself [Name]"* or *"Myself coming from [City]"*.
- **Day 14 Post-Test:** 0% reflexive subject errors. 100% of learners naturally produced *"I am [Name]"* or *"My name is [Name]"*.

#### B. Concord & Partitive Agreement
- **Day 1 Pattern:** Persistent omission of plural endings in constructions such as *"One of my friend"* and *"I have two brother"*.
- **Day 14 Post-Test:** 91% accuracy on plural nouns following partitives (*"One of my friends"*) and cardinal numbers (*"two brothers"*).

#### C. Past Tense Auxiliary Control
- **Day 1 Pattern:** Frequent double-marking of past tense: *"He did not went"* and *"Yesterday night I watched"*.
- **Day 14 Post-Test:** Learners successfully deployed *did not + base verb* (*"He did not go"*) and natural time collocations (*"Last night"*).

### 4. Direct Side-by-Side Sample Evaluations

```
PROMPT 1: "Introduce yourself and your native town."
- Day 1 Attempt: "Myself Karthik, I am coming from Madurai village." (Score: 4.0/10)
  Errors: Reflexive opening; present continuous for permanent origin.
- Day 14 Attempt: "Good morning. I am Karthik, and I come from a green village near Madurai." (Score: 9.5/10)
  Result: Natural formal greeting, correct subject pronoun, standard simple present.

PROMPT 2: "Talk about what you did yesterday evening."
- Day 1 Attempt: "Yesterday night I did not went to study." (Score: 3.5/10)
  Errors: Literal time collocation; double past tense ('did' + 'went').
- Day 14 Attempt: "Last night I revised my English grammar exercises and rested." (Score: 9.0/10)
  Result: Correct time expression 'last night', clean regular and irregular past verbs.

PROMPT 3: "Describe your family members."
- Day 1 Attempt: "I am having two brother and my sister work in bank." (Score: 4.0/10)
  Errors: Stative verb continuous misuse; missing plural -s; dropped third-person -s.
- Day 14 Attempt: "I have two brothers, and my elder sister works in a nationalized bank." (Score: 9.2/10)
  Result: Proper stative 'have', plural concord 'brothers', third-person concord 'works'.
```
