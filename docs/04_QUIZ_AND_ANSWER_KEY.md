### QUESTION PAPER

#### 1. Vocabulary Identification
**Question:** What is the English meaning of the Tamil greeting 'காலை வணக்கம்'?
- (A) Good night
- (B) Good afternoon
- (C) Good morning
- (D) Goodbye

#### 2. Adjective & Emotional Vocabulary
**Question:** Which English word translates to 'மகிழ்ச்சியான' (feeling joy or contentment)?
- (A) Angry
- (B) Tired
- (C) Sad
- (D) Happy

#### 3. Part of Speech Classification
**Question:** What part of speech is the word **'Delicious'** in the sentence *"The sambar is delicious"*?
- (A) Noun
- (B) Adjective
- (C) Verb
- (D) Preposition

#### 4. Subject-Verb Agreement (Simple Present)
**Question:** Choose the correct verb to complete: *"She ____ English with great confidence."*
- (A) speaks
- (B) speak
- (C) is speak
- (D) speaking

#### 5. Article Usage & Phonetics
**Question:** Which of the following sentences uses the indefinite article correctly?
- (A) He is a honest man.
- (B) He is an honest man.
- (C) He is the honest man without reason.
- (D) He is an doctor.

#### 6. Simple Past Tense
**Question:** Fill in the blank with the correct past tense form: *"We ____ to Chennai yesterday."*
- (A) go
- (B) gone
- (C) went
- (D) going

#### 7. Auxiliary Verb & Negative Construction
**Question:** Identify the grammatically correct negative sentence:
- (A) He did not saw me.
- (B) He did not see me.
- (C) He not saw me.
- (D) He does not saw me.

#### 8. Bilingual Translation
**Question:** Translate into standard English: *"நான் ஒரு கல்லூரி மாணவர்."*
- (A) I is a college student.
- (B) I am a college student.
- (C) Myself college student.
- (D) Me am college student.

#### 9. Sentence Correction (L1 Interference)
**Question:** Correct the frequent error: *"One of my friend is working in a hospital."*
- (A) One of my friend are working.
- (B) One of my friends is working in a hospital.
- (C) One of my friends are working.
- (D) One of friend is working.

#### 10. Conversational Politeness
**Question:** What is the polite and customary reply when someone asks *"How are you today?"*?
- (A) Why are you asking?
- (B) I am fine, thank you. How about you?
- (C) Yes, I am.
- (D) No problem at all.

| Q# | Correct Option | Correct Answer | English Pedagogical Explanation | Tamil Explanation (தமிழ் விளக்கம்) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **(C)** | Good morning | 'காலை வணக்கம்' is formally spoken before 12:00 PM. | காலை வேளையில் கூறும் வாழ்த்து 'Good morning'. |
| **2** | **(D)** | Happy | 'Happy' expresses joy and contentment. | 'Happy' என்றால் 'மகிழ்ச்சியான' என்று பொருள். |
| **3** | **(B)** | Adjective | 'Delicious' describes the culinary quality of a noun (sambar). | சுவையை விவரிக்கும் சொல்லாக வருவதால் இது பெயரடை (Adjective). |
| **4** | **(A)** | speaks | Third-person singular subjects (He/She/It) add -s to the base verb. | 'She' ஒருமை என்பதால் வினைச்சொல்லில் 's' சேர்க்க வேண்டும். |
| **5** | **(B)** | He is an honest man. | 'Honest' has a silent 'h' and begins with an 'o' vowel sound, taking 'an'. | 'Honest' உயிர் ஒலியில் தொடங்குவதால் 'an' வர வேண்டும். |
| **6** | **(C)** | went | 'Yesterday' indicates a completed past event. 'Went' is the past of 'go'. | 'Yesterday' இறந்த காலம் என்பதால் 'went' பயன்படுத்த வேண்டும். |
| **7** | **(B)** | He did not see me. | 'Did' already carries the past tense; the following main verb remains in base form. | 'Did not' வந்தால் அடுத்து 'see' என்ற மூல வினைச்சொல் மட்டுமே வரும். |
| **8** | **(B)** | I am a college student. | In English, the pronoun 'I' requires the copula verb 'am' and indefinite article 'a'. | 'I am a...' என்பதே முழுமையான இலக்கண வாக்கியம். |
| **9** | **(B)** | One of my friends is working... | 'One of my' requires a plural noun ('friends') and a singular verb ('is'). | பல நண்பர்களில் ஒருவர் என்பதால் 'friends' பன்மையும் 'is' ஒருமையாகவும் வரும். |
| **10** | **(B)** | I am fine, thank you. How about you? | Standard courteous reciprocity in conversational English. | நலம் விசாரித்து பதில் அளிப்பதே பண்பான உரையாடல் முறை. |
