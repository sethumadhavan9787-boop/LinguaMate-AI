AI Verification & Limitations Report
Formal audit of AI tools for educational deployment with Tamil ESL learners
Section 10 Capstone Deliverable
1. Empirical Findings

Across 10 benchmark sentences, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude accurately caught 95%+ of prescriptive grammatical errors. However, models without explicit bilingual grounding frequently offered generic English explanations without explaining the underlying Tamil syntactic cause.

2. Known AI Limitations

Automated tools sometimes over-correct acceptable informal expressions (e.g. flagging conversational contractions) and occasionally hallucinate grammar rules for regional Indian idioms rather than clearly explaining semantic redundancy (e.g., 'revert back').

3. Recommended Guardrails

LinguaMate AI enforces strict bilingual guardrails: curated fallback dictionaries, rule-constrained grammar verification, human-in-the-loop audit controls, and a dedicated reporting pipeline for questionable generated answers.
