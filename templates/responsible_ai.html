Ethical & Responsible AI Architecture

Human-in-the-Loop Safeguards for AI Language Education

AI is a supportive practice assistant, not an infallible authority. LinguaMate AI is engineered to empower Tamil-speaking beginners while mitigating algorithmic biases, dialect erasure, and hallucinations.

1. Cultural Nuance

Respects South Indian socio-cultural references (e.g., idli, pongal, town buses, college campus norms) rather than forcing Western cultural idioms onto beginners.

2. Dialect Awareness

Distinguishes valid Indian English expressions from actual syntactic errors. Explains standard global English alternatives without ridiculing regional communication.

3. Translation Ambiguity

Warns against literal word-for-word translation between Tamil (SOV syntax) and English (SVO syntax). Emphasizes idiomatic naturalness over machine transliteration.

4. Anti-Hallucination

Enforces explicit negative constraints in system prompts: "Do not invent grammar rules. If unsure, acknowledge uncertainty." Uses curated rule fallbacks.

5. Guarding Over-Trust

Reminds students that LLM output is probabilistic. Encourages cross-checking with teachers, dictionaries, and human mentors rather than accepting output blindly.

6. Privacy by Design

Zero personal identifying data (PII) collection. All chat history, quiz attempts, and mistake records stay entirely in local SQLite storage on the learner's own computer.

7. Human Verification

Provides human review controls across all 10 benchmark sentences. Teachers and students can mark AI responses as Verified, Uncertain, or Incorrect.

8. Active Feedback Loop

Includes an accessible "Report AI" button in every module so learners can flag questionable corrections directly to the local database audit log.
